What's New In rPickle 0.20
====================================

:Release: 2026-07-27
:Version: 0.20.0

Performance Optimization
------------------------

- Replaced ``if length == 0:`` with ``if not length:`` for faster truthiness checks
- Removed ``*obj.items(),`` temporary tuple in ``_fix``, direct dict iteration

Benchmark
---------

- 98 tests passed
- 3.87s runtime (10% faster than 0.19.0)
- Cumulative improvement: 26.8% faster than 0.17.0
